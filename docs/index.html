The ritual

Ninety seconds between you and a crackling crust.

The caramel comes in its own sachet. Sprinkle it, put the jar under the broiler for a minute and a half, then let it rest two minutes. The sugar sets into a sheet you have to break with the back of a spoon. That sound is the whole point.

How to enjoy it

The classic

In the oven, on broil

  1. Preheat your oven on broil mode.
  2. Remove the aluminium lid & sprinkle the caramel pieces on top of the Crème Brûlée.
  3. Place the dessert on the top rack of the oven & bake for 90 seconds.
  4. Cool down for 2 minutes (in order to have a thin layer of crunchy caramel)
  5. Enjoy !

The French way

With a small blowtorch

The dessert can also be caramelized with a small kitchen blowtorch. The french way of doing it, achieving the perfect caramel crust!

Sprinkle the caramel evenly, torch until golden, then rest two minutes.

Straight from the fridge

Chilled, in two steps

  1. Remove the aluminium lids & sprinkle the caramel pieces on top of the Crème Brûlée.
  2. Enjoy!

The shortcut

In the air fryer

  1. Remove the aluminium lid.
  2. Add the caramel pieces over the cream.
  3. Cook 2 minutes at 400°F (200°C).
  4. Rest 2 minutes, then enjoy!

Our story · Family-owned since 1901

The farm that grew a family

A few miles away from the iconic city of Sancerre lies a charming little village by the name of Rians: a peaceful haven where Désiré Triballat and his wife Francine settled in a little dairy farm in 1901.

Fifty years later, their grandson Hubert took over the family farm and named it Rians. In the 1990s, Hubert's son Hugues took up the rein of the company and still leads it today — because the Triballat family is all about human relationships, respect and transmission.
